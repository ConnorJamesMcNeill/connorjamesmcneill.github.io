I've had a mind for engineering since I was a boy. I've always been fascinated with understanding how the world works, which is tough to make a career out of, but engineering is pretty close.

I'm a mechanical engineering graduate from the University of Calgary. I'm currently a lab technician there, primarily supporting undergraduate labs and coursework, with research and teams as a secondary part of the role. During my studies I interned at TransCanada Turbines, then did part-time design work for EV Underground, and spent time in the Schulich Makerspace along the way.

This site is a log of the projects that curiosity has produced over the years: automotive and small-engine work, electronics, and a few things from work and school, though it's really just whatever I've remembered to photograph in recent years.

Feel free to look around, or reach out through the links in the sidebar.
